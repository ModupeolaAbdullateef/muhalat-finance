Why dividends are Halal-friendly

Dividend income represents a share of real business profits — not a fixed predetermined payment on a loan. When the underlying business is Shariah-screened, dividend investing aligns with Islamic finance principles, making it a preferred income strategy for Muslim investors.

What makes a fund Halal / Shariah-compliant?

A Shariah-compliant fund avoids companies involved in alcohol, tobacco, gambling, conventional banking (interest-based), pork products, and weapons. A Shariah Advisory Board of Islamic scholars certifies the fund and audits it regularly. Sukuk replace conventional bonds by structuring returns as rental payments or profit-sharing — not as interest on a loan.
